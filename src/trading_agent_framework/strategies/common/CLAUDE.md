# strategies/common/ -- code shared between strategies

> Nested `CLAUDE.md`; project-wide rules are in the root `CLAUDE.md`. Paths are relative to `src/trading_agent_framework/` unless they start with `tests/`, `scripts/` or `docs/`.

## Architecture

- `strategies/common/` -- code shared between strategies, imported from there (no re-export shims; docs under `docs/superpowers/` still name the old locations): `scoring.py` (**pure** momentum scoring: `compute_return_from_prices`, `annualized_volatility`, `momentum_score`, `apply_filters`, `momentum_inputs`, then `score_stock` -> `MomentumRow` (`None` when filtered out or without a volatility, which cross_momentum would still rank but bull_bear cannot gate on `max_volatility` or show in its fact sheet) and `rank` -> `RankedRow`), `stops.py` (`release_stop(strategy, stop_order_id, *, expected_cancels, wait_seconds, warn_prefix)` -> `Release(status, order, error)`, `StopRelease` GONE / FILLED / RELEASED / FAILED: cancels a working stop and waits before an exit sell, shared by the earnings_drift and vwap_pullback desks; the id is in `expected_cancels` during the cancel and **discarded again on a cancel error or a timeout**, so a late CANCELED reaches the desk's stop path; a failed lookup is FAILED, never GONE; each desk books the partial fill and keeps its own return values), `rebalancer.py` (`in_flight_quantities(open_orders)` -> (buys, sells), the unfilled quantity per symbol of open orders, also used by cross_momentum; `submit_buy(strategy, symbol, quantity, price, *, min_trade=0.0, decimals=6)` -> (order, quantity sent), the one buy path of the Rebalancer, cross_momentum (`_send`) and earnings_drift (`Desk._buy`): a buy `Strategy.submit_order` refuses for money (`InsufficientFundsError`) is sent once more at `exc.max_quantity` floored to `decimals` (0: whole shares), unless that is nothing or worth less than `min_trade` at the caller's `price`, which re-raises the refusal with no order sent; a second refusal propagates (why: callers size the last buy to exactly `spendable_cash()` at a price read before the sells and their 30 s fill wait, so live any uptick refused it and the stock was dropped); `Rebalancer`, typed against the `RebalanceParams` protocol: `parking_symbol`, `rebalance_band`, `min_trade_pct` (a strategy's `cash_buffer` only shrinks its target weights; the cash kept back from the buys is the framework's 2%); sells first, then buys, then SHV; **a buy the cash cannot pay for is funded by parking first (SHV above nothing but what the shortfall needs), then by trimming the stocks above their targets, inside their band too, the largest excess first and only by the shortfall (`trim to fund the buys`; waited on in paper/live like any sell; a stock this run already sold is never trimmed again: `submit` tallies every sell, accepted or unknown, in a per-rebalance `sold` that `sellable`/`value_of` subtract and that stays apart from the earlier runs' `outgoing`; the 2026-11-18 backtest sent 22 second sells of a stock already sold in the same run: the broker accepted 18, which left the ETF under its target, and refused 4 as "insufficient position"): a book that is always invested has no parking and no cash, and with every other stock just inside its band a buy over the band had nothing to pay for it, so the rebalancer sent no order (the 2026-11-18 sector_pods backtest abandoned 8 weeks, the last 3 in a row aborted the run); `Rebalancer.refused` counts the orders the broker refused in the last `rebalance`, so a caller can tell a refusal from a run that had nothing to send**; **long positions only** (`Rebalancer._long_positions`: IBKR and Tastytrade report a short as a POSITIVE quantity with `side=SHORT`, so a `quantity > 0` filter alone counted it as a holding and a "sell" of it opened more short; a short is skipped and `rebalance` warns about it); a sell's log line ends with an estimated P&L against the position's `avg_fill_price` at the quoted price (`sell 100 B @ $25.00 (not in the target portfolio, est. P&L +$500.00 / +25.0%)`; omitted when the broker reports no cost or the price is unknown); **the cash invariant**: the buys are sized from `strategy.spendable_cash()`, read once after the stock sells (the account at the start gives only the portfolio value); it reads the account FIRST and the open orders second, each order for its unfilled part only, so a sell of this run that fills within seconds is counted once (in cash, or still open) and an earlier fill is never credited again -- reading the open orders before the account double-counted it (a bug that hit congress_trades and earnings_drift); the parking sell and the funding trims add their estimated proceeds to it, each buy takes its cost off, every buy (parking too) is capped by `spendable_cash()` read again just before it (a backtest's buying power nets the earlier buys' fees, which the estimate does not, so a buy sized to the last dollar was refused), a buy refused by the framework (`InsufficientFundsError`) is retried once at the most that fits (`submit_buy`; each buy then deducts the quantity really sent), and one refused again or too small to retry lowers the running figure to its `spendable` (`min`, never raises it: an unknown buy's cost stays spent) and the next stock is sized on it, a broker refusal for buying power (Alpaca) resyncs to the broker's figure less 2%, and a failed `spendable_cash()` (account or quote) raises like the account read it replaced, so the pipeline abandons the review with the sells already sent in `placed`; that read happens in paper/live only once the stock sells have FILLED, and the first buy only once the parking (SHV) sell and the funding trims have filled too (every sell of this run is in the waited list; on a cash account buying power credits the SHV proceeds only on the fill, so the first buy's fresh `spendable_cash()` saw no money and skipped every buy) (`strategy.wait_for_orders_execution`, `Rebalancer(fill_wait_seconds=30)`; a live broker credits buying power on a fill, not on acceptance: read 350 ms early, the 2026-10-27 bull_bear VSXY buy was skipped with "No cash left for buying"; a timeout warns and sizes on buying power as it stands; a backtest never waits, its buying power already credits a submitted sell and a wait would only move the simulated clock). The invariant does NOT cover an open BUY that fills between `spendable_cash()`'s account read and its open-order read (its cost is counted nowhere); `buying_power`, one side of its `min()`, bounds that on a cash account only; pinned by two live-fill tests in `tests/strategies/common/test_common_rebalancer.py`; **an unknown outcome** (`BrokerUncertainError`, Tastytrade): the order is kept by the broker, never resent and never counted as refused (no buying-power resync): `PlacedOrder.confirmed=False`, an unknown buy's cost stays deducted from the available cash, an unknown sell (stock or SHV) adds no proceeds and is not waited on; **A "phantom" unknown order (the POST failed, Tastytrade never created it) is settled before every tick.** It produces no stream event and stays tracked as SUBMITTED (unprocessed, so `get_active_orders()` returns it, and a phantom BUY would be counted as in flight and a phantom SELL would make the holding unsellable), so the executor calls `Broker.settle_unknown_orders()` before each tick (`core/CLAUDE.md`): `TastytradeBroker` looks the order up, adopts it if it exists and fails it after two misses at least 30 s apart, never resending it. With daily ticks a phantom from Tuesday's rebalance fails by Thursday, before the next Tuesday. Fallback if it ever stays: check the Tastytrade account and restart the strategy (the tracker is in memory).), `portfolio.py` (**pure** `TargetPortfolio`, `target_portfolio`), `sessions.py` (**pure** `completed_bars`, `parse_rebalance_time`), `yahoo_daily_bars.py` (`YahooDailyBars`, the one batched Yahoo download of paper/live daily bars; the `BarsSource` protocol; `fetch_usable_batch(source, universe, *, extras, today, held, min_coverage, retry_delays, sleep, on_retry)`, the retry and coverage check cross_momentum and bull_bear share: a batch is unusable when the lookup fails (`Yahoo lookup failed (...)`), covers under `min_coverage` of the universe only (`extras` such as SPY are requested but not counted) or misses a held stock that is in the universe (`held` is read only once coverage passes; a holding outside the universe is ignored); `on_retry(problem, delay)` then `sleep(delay)` after each failure, `UnusableBatch(problem)` after the last; each caller keeps its own log wording and its own sleep), `sector_provider.py` (`SectorProvider`: yfinance sectors cached per instance, `"UNKNOWN"` on any failure, not point-in-time). Used by cross_momentum, bill_ackman and bull_bear.
- `daily_bars.py` (`DailyBars`, `YahooBars`, `GateBars`, `DailySeries`, `DataUnavailable`, `HISTORY_BARS`; moved from `bull_bear/market_data.py`): a strategy's daily bars, Yahoo in paper/live and the backtest gate otherwise. Used by bull_bear and sector_pods.
- `trade_desk.py`, `handoff.py`, `run_log.py` (moved from congress_trades, shared with it and insider_trades): `TradeDesk(strategy, params: DeskParams, *, log_tag)` (`DeskParams` protocol: `rebalance_band`, `min_trade_pct`, `order_wait_seconds`) is the only order code of those strategies (a buy never beyond `Strategy.spendable_cash()`, its weight and minimum-size checks on top), `log_tag` prefixes its log lines (`[congress_trades] ...`) and is required; it is built with `begin_run(target | None, traded)`: with no target (`has_target` False) `place_order` refuses with `no target yet: call submit_target first` before reading the account and `audit()` has nothing to compare against, until `set_target` installs one WITHOUT clearing the orders placed or `traded`. `handoff.py` imports `HandoffError` from `agents/submit.py` (with `SubmitRecorder`, which moved there) and holds `OrderView`, `OrderReport`, `validate_report`, the public field readers `items`, `key`, `required_text`/`optional_text` (take the field name), `text` (the `reason` field, `required=`), `weight(item, ticker, *, example="0.08")`, `trim_text` moved to `agents/tools/text.py` (an over-long free text cut to the longest run of whole sentences that fits, else at a word with an ellipsis, never reworded; used by the length gate) and `covering(raw, expected, *, what, field="symbol", unknown, missing)` (one `(key, item)` per expected key in submission order, all key/unknown/duplicate checks first, then `missing`; used by bull_bear only, because bill_ackman, congress and insider interleave per-item checks with the key checks, so `covering` would change which error the model sees first) (no `from __future__ import annotations`: it sits next to tool closures; the base of all four strategies' recorders, `SubmitRecorder`, is `agents/submit.py`'s); `run_log.py` holds `RunLog(path | Callable[[], Path | None] | None, *, what="run log").append(record)` (the one JSONL run log: `runs.jsonl`, `reviews.jsonl`, `trades.jsonl`, `decisions.jsonl`; a callable path is read at every append; it serializes before opening and never raises on I/O or serialization, logging `<what> <path> could not be written`). `json_state.py` holds `atomic_write_json(path, payload, *, log, what="state") -> bool` (serialize first, mkstemp, replace; logs `<what> could not be saved to <path>` and returns False; also used by the bill_ackman annual-figures and split caches), the state validators `is_int`/`is_weight`/`valid_strings`/`valid_weights`, `InvalidState` and `JsonStateStore[S]` (a missing file is `empty()`, an unreadable or invalid one warns and is empty; `decode(raw)` gets the whole dict and raises `InvalidState` to reject it; `accepts` lists the readable versions, `save` always writes `version`; `fresh=True` wipes in the constructor, so a strategy builds `StateStore(self.state_file("<name>"), fresh=self.is_backtesting)` in `initialize`). Each strategy's `StateStore` subclasses it.
- `cooldowns.py` (**pure** `advance_cooldowns(current, *, started, length)`, used by bill_ackman and bull_bear).
- `news.py` (`require_news_provider(broker, who)`, the fail-fast news-source check of sector_pods and bull_bear: `ConfigurationError` when `broker.news_provider()` raises `BrokerError` (the reason is kept) or returns `None`; `who` is the subject and verb, `"the pods need"` / `"the researcher needs"`; the callers turn it into `FatalStrategyError`. earnings_drift's scanner and `agents/tools/news.py` read the provider at run time and degrade instead: not the same check).
- `pipeline.py` (the skeleton of the four agent pipelines: `AckmanPipeline`, `BullBearPipeline`, `CongressPipeline`, `InsiderPipeline`): `ReviewPipeline[S]` is a template. Its `run()` loads the state, builds the record (`date`, `run_id`, then `RECORD_FLAG`: `abandoned` False/True for the review pipelines, `outcome` None/`"abandoned"` for congress and insider), calls `_begin()` (reset what a failure is attributed to; a no-op for bull_bear, which resets `_stage` in `_review`) and the body `_run(now, state, record) -> RunOutcome`. An abandonment is a `StageAbandoned(stage, message)` (stage = its `.stage`), a `DATA_ERRORS` exception (stage `filings`) or a `BrokerError`/`BacktestError` (stage `execution` when `_executing()` else `broker`; `ConfigurationError` propagates): it bumps the streak, logs `<LOG_PREFIX><WHAT> abandoned at the <stage> stage (streak N): <error>`, saves what `_on_abandoned(state, streak, stage)` returns and appends `{**record, <flag>: abandoned, stage, error, abandoned_streak, **extra}`. The four `test_abandoned_record_unchanged` tests pin the log line, the record (key order included) and the saved state. Also here: `AgentLike`/`AgentLookup` (bull_bear's subclass `AgentLike` adds `max_steps`), `RunOutcome(completed, abandoned_streak)`. Concrete pending-trade steps only congress_trades and insider_trades call (they take the `TradeDesk`/state as arguments; bill_ackman and bull_bear gain nothing): `PendingTrade`/`valid_pending`/`decode_pending` (the state modules import them), `_orders_record(desk)`, `_at_target(before)` (logs the at-target line, True when nothing is off target), `_audited(desk, record)` (the after-audit record and warnings), `_give_up_pending`, `_finish_pending` and `_abandoned_with_pending` (the `_on_abandoned` body). Each strategy keeps its own log lines around them. `_run_stage`, `retry_prompt` and each body stay in the strategy: the four differ too much to share them.
- `universe_history.py` (pure apart from `save`/`load`): the point-in-time stock universe cross_momentum and bull_bear backtest on. `UniverseHistory` (`built`, `pool_date`, `top_n`, `min_price`, `min_dollar_volume`, `lookback`, `months`: "YYYY-MM" -> symbols, best first; plus `pool_size`, `unanswered`, `bars_absent`, which describe the latest build's pool and default to 0/empty for older files) with `on(day)` (the list of `day`'s month; **a missing month raises `UniverseHistoryError`, never another month's list**), `covers`, `union` (every symbol of a window, for the preload) and `save`/`load` (`save` writes a temp file and renames it). `monthly_lists` ranks each month on data dated **before its first session only**: the mean of `traded_close * traded_volume` (split-unadjusted, from `parse_yahoo_frame`) over the 63 sessions before it, kept when the last such traded close is at least `min_price` ($10) and the mean at least `min_dollar_volume` ($20M) (`universe_params.py`), top 1,200. A symbol needs 63 prior rows and its last one within the 10 sessions before the month's first (`_MAX_STALE_SESSIONS`), so a stock that stopped trading is not ranked on its old volume. Why: the universe file is built today and holds today's winners, so a backtest on it trades stocks that had already won. **Residual bias: stocks delisted inside the window are absent from Yahoo, so from every list.** The build is `ensure_universe_history` in `batch_us_stock_universe.py` (`strategies/cross_momentum/CLAUDE.md`).
- `universe_params.py`: `MIN_PRICE`, `MIN_DOLLAR_VOLUME`, `MIN_MARKET_CAP`, the one home of the universe's liquidity thresholds; the batch module filters on them and cross_momentum's `CONFIG` reads them (change them here only). `universe_history.py` also holds `market_day` (a backtest bound's market-time date), `window_symbols(history, start, end, exclude=)` (the window's union, for the preload) and `month_universe(history, day, exclude=)` (a review's list, in rank order; a missing month raises `FatalStrategyError`, since the executor swallows anything else as a failed tick).
- `batch_us_stock_universe.py` (`uv run batch-universe`; moved here from cross_momentum, it no longer imports any strategy): the live universe batch and the point-in-time history build (`ensure_universe_history`, `build_history_pool`, `HistoryPool`). Its shared yfinance state is module-level (`rate_limit_gate`, `_semaphore`); tests replace `rate_limit_gate` with `monkeypatch.setattr` and the code reads the name at call time. `_etf_symbols(..., strict=)` is the IWB/IWM/VTI union: lenient for the live batch (`get_extra_universe_symbols`), strict for the history pool. `download_batch`, `HISTORY_BATCH_SIZE` and `MAX_ABSENT_FRACTION` are public because `universe_backtest_data.py` uses them.
- `universe_backtest_data.py`: `UniverseBacktestData`, the batched, retried Yahoo preload of a point-in-time backtest, and `universe_parameters` (the `parameters["universe"]` record).
- **The `min_price` / `min_dollar_volume` filters read what a stock actually traded at.** `momentum_inputs` / `score_stock` take `traded_closes=` and `traded_volumes=` (`yahoo.traded_series(frame)`, cut after `completed_bars(...).tail(...)`, `None` unless the frame has both `traded_close` and `traded_volume`); with both, `price` is the last traded close and `avg_dollar_volume` the mean of each of the last 20 sessions' traded close x traded volume (a last close x average volume would mix pre- and post-split share counts for 20 sessions after a split), while returns and volatility always come from the adjusted closes (so momentum is unchanged). Without them (an Alpaca frame, an `auto_adjust=True` parse) the adjusted series feed the filters as before. Why: the adjusted close is split- and dividend-adjusted back from today, so the filters saw prices no one ever traded at. NVDA printed about $20 in early 2015 but about $0.50 adjusted, so the $10 filter dropped it until mid-2020; a stock yielding 3% a year looks about 35% less liquid 15 years back. `YahooDailyBars` (paper/live) downloads the same `auto_adjust=False, actions=True` shape as the backtest (`backtesting/CLAUDE.md`).
