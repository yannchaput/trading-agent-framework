# strategies/cross_momentum/ -- momentum ranking, no LLM

> Nested `CLAUDE.md`; project-wide rules are in the root `CLAUDE.md`. Paths are relative to `src/trading_agent_framework/` unless they start with `tests/`, `scripts/` or `docs/`.

## Architecture

- `cross_momentum/` (no LLM).

## Gotchas

- **cross_momentum decides on completed sessions and rebalances at 12:00 ET.** Live daily bars include the forming session's bar, so a Tuesday decision used to depend on the hour it ran: on 2026-10-06 the 09:42 ET run read the risk overlay NORMAL and a 15:36 restart read CRITICAL. Every computation (scores, filters, breadth, the overlay's SPY series, the parking sleeve's GLD/IEF 200-day SMA test) now drops the bar dated today (`strategies/common/sessions.completed_bars`, a no-op in backtests where the data gate already hides it) and keeps 300 completed bars; trims and buys are sized at the last trade (`get_last_price`), not at the completed close. The overlay joins its series on session DATE (`utils.close_series`; live bars are stamped at midnight, backtest bars at the close): it used to align them by position, and half the series ending a session early halved beta and correlation. The cause of that shift was never found; a warning names any target whose last bar date differs from SPY's. `parameters["rebalance_time"]` ("12:00", ET) sets `iteration_start_time`, validated to 09:30 <= t < 15:59 by `strategies/common/sessions.parse_rebalance_time` (a half-day Tuesday starting at or after 12:59 loses that week's rebalance), so the daily equity sample is taken at 12:00 too. A restart on a Tuesday after 12:00 rebalances again (deliberately unguarded).
- **cross_momentum reads its daily bars from Yahoo in every mode.** Alpaca IEX bars carry ~5% of consolidated volume (median; measured 2026-10-08 on the 1200-symbol universe), skip a thin stock's quiet days and close at the last IEX trade, so the unchanged `apply_filters` (`strategies/common/scoring.py`, `min_dollar_volume` 20M) passed 307 symbols live against ~1,140 in a Yahoo backtest, live never saw KOD, GSAT, PACS, IOVA..., and ranks ran on other prices than the backtest's (a decade backtest at the IEX-equivalent 400M: CAGR 34% -> 19%, last year +57% -> +35%). Paper/live now load ONE Yahoo batch per scan (`strategies/common/yahoo_daily_bars.YahooDailyBars`, shared with bull_bear: the backtest's own download and parse, `backtesting/data/yahoo.py`'s `yfinance_download`/`extract_ticker_frame`/`parse_yahoo_frame`, split- and dividend-adjusted, stamped at the session close; the universe plus SPY plus the sleeve's trend assets, ~10 s instead of ~12 min of rate-limited Alpaca requests) into `vars.yahoo_bars`, and `CrossMomentumStrategy.get_historical_prices` (an override) serves every daily read from it: the stock scan (filter inputs, scores, ranks, breadth, weights), the risk overlay's SPY and the sleeve's GLD/IEF trend. The decision code is the backtest's own, unchanged; a symbol Yahoo has no bars for gets None, never an Alpaca fallback on other data; minute bars and everything non-`day` still go to the framework. The Alpaca rate-limiter wait now sits next to the one Alpaca branch of the override. Orders and sizing (`get_last_price`, positions, cash) still go through Alpaca. `CrossMomentumStrategy(bars_source=...)` injects a source; a backtest has none. A batch is unusable when the download fails, when Yahoo covers under half the UNIVERSE (`_MIN_YAHOO_COVERAGE`; SPY and the trend assets do not count toward it), or when it misses a HELD universe stock (an unranked holding would be sold as "not ranked"; a holding outside the universe is never requested and is sold by design). An unusable batch is fetched again after each of `_YAHOO_RETRY_DELAYS` (60 s, 180 s, through `Strategy.sleep`), then the scan is skipped ("holding current positions"): a transient 429 must not cost the week's only rebalance. A symbol whose Yahoo data cannot be parsed is left out with a warning. The market regime is fetched the same way (`CrossMomentumStrategy._refresh_regime` pulls SPY from the bars source on its own, because the executor refreshes the regime before the open, when `vars.yahoo_bars` is empty: until 2026-10-09 that gave no regime at all in paper/live, logged as "fewer than 273 daily bars"). `rebalance` (a `try/finally` around `_rebalance_book`) releases `vars.yahoo_bars` when it ends, whatever happens: it is their last reader, so no stale bars outlive the scan. Yahoo's bar dated today is partial while the session is open and is dropped by `completed_bars`, as before. `min_market_cap` in `parameters.py` is still not enforced by `apply_filters` (the universe batch pre-filters it); do not add a gate without a decision.
