# strategies/insider_trades/ -- Form 4 insider trades of a fixed watchlist

> Nested `CLAUDE.md`; project-wide rules are in the root `CLAUDE.md`. Paths are relative to `src/trading_agent_framework/` unless they start with `tests/`, `scripts/` or `docs/`.

## Architecture

- `insider_trades/` (`InsiderTradesStrategy`, registered as `"insider_trades"`): a researcher (one run per changed stock that has trades in its window; a changed stock with an empty window gets a note written by code, no agent run) then one trader (two submits) over the Form 4 filings of the `InsiderParams.watchlist` stocks. `sleeptime = "1D"` and `iteration_start_time = 10:00`, but `InsiderPipeline` acts only when a stock's filings changed. Modules:
  - `agent_insider_trades.py` -- the strategy: `initialize` builds `Form4Source` (`SecEdgarClient`, cache `cache/sec/`), `InsiderFacts`, `InsiderRecorder`, `TradeDesk(self, params, log_tag="insider_trades")` and the three agents (`researcher`, `trader`, `trader_retry`); `load_window()` is the pipeline's `(as_of, since)` for a Form 4 load; `run_backtesting` records the merged `start`/`end` in `_backtest_start`/`_backtest_end` for it.
  - `parameters.py` (`InsiderParams`, every threshold and the watchlist), `state.py` (`StateStore`: processed accessions, per-stock notes and in-window accessions, target, `traded`, `pending_trade`, abandoned streak, in `data/insider_trades_state_<mode>.json`), `pipeline.py` (`InsiderPipeline`, the daily check), `handoff.py` (`submit_note`, `submit_target`, `submit_trade_report`, validated by `InsiderRecorder`, no `from __future__ import annotations`), `tools.py` (`insider_research_tools(strategy, facts, on_fetch)`: `get_insider_trades`, `read_form4`, also no `from __future__ import annotations`), `facts.py` (`InsiderFacts`: the clock-gated, labelled, windowed view of one market date), `prompts.py`.
  - `form4/` -- **pure** except `source.py`: `parse.py` (Form 4 XML -> `Form4Filing` / `Form4Trade`), `classify.py` (routine / opportunistic / unclassified), `signal.py` (window, overrides, `StockFacts`, `SignalClass`), `source.py` (`Form4Source`: the only SEC I/O; `load(as_of, since)` once, `known(as_of)` cheap).
  - The order desk is `common/trade_desk.py`'s `TradeDesk` and `RunLog` is `common/run_log.py`'s, as in `congress_trades`.

## Gotchas

- **Every filing is keyed on its FILING date, strictly before today's market date.** A Form 4 is due two business days after the trade, and a backtest that keyed on the trade date would look into the future. `Form4Source.known` applies the rule, and `InsiderFacts.known()` applies it again to whatever a source returns (the research-tool clock rule: the cutoff comes from `strategy.clock.now()`, never the wall clock). The signal window is `[today - signal_window_days, today)` on the filing date.
- **Only officers and directors count, and a joint filing is booked once.** A Form 4 lists one set of transaction rows for several reporting owners; the rows are attributed to the first listed owner who is a director or an officer, the others are named as co-filers, and a filing with no insider owner (a 10% holder alone) yields no trades. Only open-market `P` and `S` rows are trades; every other code and every derivative row is counted and dropped. A filing whose issuer CIK is not the watchlist company's (the company as a reporting owner of another issuer's shares) is dropped by `Form4Source` and counted in `LoadReport.other_issuer`. Form `4/A` and `5` are ignored.
- **Labels are per trade, by code; the researcher only verifies them.** Routine = the insider traded in a common month in each of the `history_years` calendar years before the trade's own year; opportunistic = traded in each of them but never in one common month; unclassified = a year is empty. A 10b5-1 plan trade is routine whatever the history, and an override of one is refused (`apply_overrides` also skips it). An override names an insider and a `from_label` it still has non-plan trades of, with a reason, and the researcher must have called `get_insider_trades` for that stock first. Dollars and fractions of the holding are never recomputed by an agent.
- **The Form 4 history reaches back by `load_window()`, not by the signal window.** `since` is January 1 of `(reference day - (signal_window_days + 10)).year - history_years`, the reference day being the backtest start's market date (paper/live: today's). A trade filed in early January was made the previous December, and its label needs `history_years` full years before THAT year; a plain `start.year - history_years` would leave the oldest year empty and mislabel the trade unclassified (default window start 2025-09-29 gives 2022-01-01, and so does 2026-01-05). `as_of` is the run's end in a backtest (a bare-midnight end covers that whole day, as in `earnings_drift`), `clock.now()` in paper/live; nothing may call `InsiderFacts.known()` on a new market date before `pipeline.run()` has loaded the source, so `initialize` does no facts call.
- **Signal classes decide the weights; only OPPORTUNISTIC trades move them.** `buying` = an opportunistic purchase of at least `min_buy_holding_fraction` of the insider's holding; else `selling` = opportunistic sales exceed purchases and one insider sold at least `min_sell_holding_fraction`; else `none` (a changed stock with nothing in its window gets a note written by code, with no agent run). `submit_target` enforces in code: every watchlist stock gets one weight between `min_weight` and `max_position_weight`, summing to at most `max_total_weight`; a buying stock never gets less than a none or a selling stock and a selling one never more than a none stock; with no signal anywhere every weight is the even weight; a weight off even needs a reason.
- **Act only on change, decided in code.** With no stock changed since the last COMPLETED run and no pending trade, no agent runs and no order is sent (a "nothing_new" line in `runs.jsonl`). A stock changed when a P/S filing it did not know then is now known, when a filing in its window then has left it, or on the first run; the stocks that did not change reuse their stored note. A trade the trading stage could not finish is kept as `pending_trade` and only `trader_retry` re-runs the trading stage against the stored target on later days (`max_trade_retries`), never re-sending an open order; a change replaces it. An abandoned stage (no valid submission) trades nothing and leaves the filings "new"; a backtest aborts with `FatalStrategyError` after `max_consecutive_abandoned` in a row, paper and live never.
- **The trader makes two submits and only the trading agents can order.** `submit_target` (trader only) arms the report and answers with the shortfalls; then orders through `place_order` / `check_orders` (the `TradeDesk`, which bounds every order: target weights, the SMALLER of `buying_power` and cash plus this run's sell proceeds, sells only of a target stock or of a position this strategy ordered before, sells first), then `submit_trade_report`. `trader_retry` has the same tools without `submit_target`. The researcher has no market-data, account or order tool. The prompts take their numbers (floor, cap, total, even weight) from the `InsiderParams` and never state how often the strategy runs.
- **Verified against real filings (Task 6 checkpoint, `scripts/tests/smoke_insider_trades.py`, real SEC).** AAPL and JPM both loaded with no failure; two forms were ignored (a `4/A` and a `5`); 16 filings were dropped as another issuer (the issuer filter is needed in practice); each ticker had 141 P/S trades since 2023-01-01, almost all sales, and the 10b5-1 plan ones label as routine; the cached file is raw `ownershipDocument` XML and its layout matches the synthetic fixtures. Two real filings are kept as fixtures with a parse test each: `tests/strategies/insider_trades/form4/fixtures/real_aapl_plan_2023.xml` (a 10b5-1 sale with M and F rows, `aff10b5One=1`) and `real_multi_owner_jpm.xml` in the same directory (two 10% owners, no insider, another issuer).
- **Known limitation: a ticker that keeps failing to load.** In paper/live a ticker whose Form 4s keep failing to load keeps its stored class, including an expired buying/selling tilt, until it loads again.
- **Known limitation: Tastytrade unknown-outcome submit.** The shared desk answers a `BrokerUncertainError` as "refused by the broker" (see `brokers/CLAUDE.md` "KNOWN LIMITATION"); the order stays tracked, so a buy beyond target is still refused.
